# 💊 Skill: medirx
> Medications — active prescriptions (read-only).

1. **Trigger**: Use `get_medications` when the patient asks about their current medications, prescriptions, or dosages.
2. **Read-only**: This skill retrieves data only. Prescriptions are managed by the doctor — never create, modify, or cancel prescriptions through this assistant.
3. **Safety**: Always cross-reference medications when providing OTC recommendations (see MEDICATION GUIDANCE).
