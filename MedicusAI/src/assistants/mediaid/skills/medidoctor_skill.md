# 👨‍⚕️ Skill: medidoctor
> Doctor profile — name, specialty, and contact information.

1. **Trigger**: Use `get_doctor_profile_details` when the patient asks "Who is my doctor?", needs to confirm their doctor's specialty, or requires doctor contact details.
2. **Restriction**: Returns profile data only. For appointment availability, use `medibooking`. For clinical records, use `mediclinic`.
